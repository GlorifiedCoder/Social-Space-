# Phase 3 — Notes and Personal Knowledge Library

## Goal
Turn Notes into a durable, useful personal knowledge library.

## Deliverables
- tags;
- collections;
- saved content references (URL, title, source platform, optional note);
- search and filters;
- safe link validation and opening;
- duplicate reference handling;
- export and delete controls;
- tests and SQLite migrations.

## Constraints
- Do not download or redistribute platform media without permission.
- Do not transcribe or summarize content unless the source and method permit it.
- Preserve source attribution and original links.
- Keep the app usable offline for local notes.

## Acceptance
Notes survive restart and schema upgrades. Search, edit, export, and deletion work. Add unit, widget, and repository tests. Update roadmap and changelog.
