# Phase 5 — Settings and Personalization

## Goal
Make user customization consistent and persistent across the app.

## Deliverables
- per-platform visibility and ordering;
- home startup preference (last-used remains default);
- themes and accessibility settings;
- user-created profiles/modes such as Study, Work, News, Entertainment;
- profile-specific platform visibility, content preferences, and notifications;
- reset/export/delete settings;
- clear precedence rules for global vs profile vs platform settings.

## Product truth
Content filters apply only to content My Social Space fetches and displays through an approved or app-owned experience. They cannot alter another platform's native recommendation algorithm.

## Acceptance
Settings persist, conflicts have deterministic precedence, and reset/export/delete behavior is tested and documented.
